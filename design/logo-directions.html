SITERESOLVE / IDENTITY

Three logo directions

Each direction is a symbol paired with the SiteResolve wordmark in Inter Bold, the typeface the site already uses. They are shown at display size, in the site header, as a browser tab icon and as an app icon.

Current logo for reference:

DIRECTION A

Closure

A ring that is not quite closed, with a tick breaking out through the gap. It reads as a defect being closed off, and the tick is the one place the blue appears.

  • Strength: says what the product does, verification, without words.
  • Risk: ticks are common in task apps, so the ring carries the identity.

DIRECTION B

Evidence

Camera viewfinder corners around a tick. It points to the moment on site when someone photographs a defect, and to the proof that it was put right.

  • Strength: the most site-specific of the three, tied to photographic evidence.
  • Risk: the corners get fine at 16 pixels, so the favicon may need a heavier cut.

DIRECTION C

Monogram

The SR letters redrawn from one stroke weight on a solid tile, like lettering on a drawing sheet. It keeps the initials people may already recognise from the current logo.

  • Strength: the least change, and the tile makes a strong app icon.
  • Risk: initials alone say little about the product.
