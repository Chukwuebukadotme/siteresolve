About SiteResolve

Defect management should not depend on scattered updates.

SiteResolve is focused on one practical problem: keeping the full path from reported defect to verified resolution clear and accessible.

Why SiteResolve exists

Construction and property teams often manage defects across photographs, calls, emails, messaging applications and spreadsheets. Each tool may hold part of the record, but no single place shows the full state of the issue.

SiteResolve is designed to connect the report, responsible parties, corrective work, supporting evidence and final decision.

Principles

  1. 01

    Capture useful information

    A defect report should contain enough context for another person to understand the issue and act on it.

  2. 02

    Make ownership visible

    Every open issue should show who is responsible for the next action and when it is due.

  3. 03

    Keep the evidence connected

    Photographs, files, comments and decisions should remain attached to the relevant issue.

  4. 04

    Verify before closure

    Completed work should be reviewed before an issue is considered closed.

  5. 05

    Support the people on site

    The product should remain clear and usable on phones, tablets and desktop devices.

  6. 06

    Use records responsibly

    Permissions, activity history and reporting should make information easier to manage without creating unnecessary complexity.

Designed for the built environment.

SiteResolve is intended for construction companies, property managers, facilities teams, maintenance teams, inspectors, contractors and subcontractors.

It can support a small contractor managing a few active jobs or a larger organisation coordinating issues across several sites.
