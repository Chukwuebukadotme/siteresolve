The problem

Defect management breaks down between steps.

A defect may begin as a photograph on one phone, become a task in an email and end up as a status in a spreadsheet. The evidence, responsibility and final decision are often stored in different places.

SiteResolve keeps the complete record together. Teams can see what was reported, who owns the next action, what work was completed and whether the resolution has been accepted.

How it works

Four steps from discovery to closure.

  1. 01Step 1: Report

    Record the defect where it is found.

    Add a description, photographs, location, category and priority from a phone, tablet or desktop. The report creates one reference for the issue.

  2. 02Step 2: Assign

    Give the next action a clear owner.

    Assign the issue to an employee, contractor or subcontractor. Add a due date and keep instructions, files and comments with the record.

  3. 03Step 3: Resolve

    Record the work and supporting evidence.

    The assignee updates the issue, adds completion notes and submits photographs or documents showing what was done.

  4. 04Step 4: Verify

    Check the work before closing the issue.

    An authorised reviewer accepts the resolution, rejects it or returns it for further work. The final decision is added to the issue history.

Representative workflow

During a handover inspection, a site manager finds that a fire door does not close correctly.

  1. 1

    Report

    The site manager photographs the door, records the floor and room, marks the issue as high priority and adds a short description.

  2. 2

    Assign

    The issue is assigned to the door subcontractor with a due date and repair instruction.

  3. 3

    Resolve

    The subcontractor adjusts the door, adds completion notes and uploads photographs showing the completed work.

  4. 4

    Verify

    The site manager reviews the evidence, checks the door on site and verifies the resolution.

  5. 5

    Close

    The issue is closed with the complete report, evidence and decision history retained.

One record shows what was found, who was responsible, what was done and who approved the result.

Frequently asked questions

What is SiteResolve?{{icon:chev}}

SiteResolve is a defect-management platform for reporting, assigning, resolving and verifying issues across construction projects, properties and facilities.

Who is SiteResolve for?{{icon:chev}}

It is designed for construction companies, property managers, facilities teams, maintenance teams, inspectors, contractors and subcontractors.

What can be recorded on an issue?{{icon:chev}}

An issue can include a description, photographs, files, location, category, priority, assignee, due date, comments, resolution evidence and verification decision.

Can contractors update assigned issues?{{icon:chev}}

SiteResolve is designed to let authorised external contractors view assigned work, add updates and submit resolution evidence without giving them access to unrelated records.

Does SiteResolve support mobile work?{{icon:chev}}

The reporting and update workflows are designed for phones, tablets and desktop devices.

Can teams work with limited connectivity?{{icon:chev}}

SiteResolve is designed to support essential capture in low-connectivity conditions and synchronise updates when a connection is restored.

Can managers report across several sites?{{icon:chev}}

Cross-site views and reporting are intended to help managers review open, overdue, awaiting-verification and completed issues.

Does SiteResolve integrate with other systems?{{icon:chev}}

SiteResolve is being structured to support integrations. Specific integrations will be listed after they have been confirmed.
